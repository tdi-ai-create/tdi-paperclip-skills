# TDI Grant Writing Standards
**Updated July 22, 2026. No application ships without meeting every requirement below.**

---

## What We Deliver

TDI delivers **complete, ready-to-submit application packages**. The client copies, pastes, and clicks submit. They never write a word, look up a number, or make a decision. If they have to think, we failed.

---

## Before You Start Drafting

### 1. Research the actual application form
Open the real application. Document every field name, character limit, and required attachment. Never guess. If you cannot access the form, flag it and stop. Do not draft against assumed fields.

### 2. Pull the school's TDI contract
Check what services are on the contract (Hub memberships, coaching visits, virtual sessions, books). The grant budget MUST fund contract line items. Never invent unrelated items like "classroom supplies" or "manipulatives."

### 3. Maximize the grant amount
If the grant allows $5,000, request $5,000. Never leave money on the table. Calculate how many TDI services the maximum covers and build the budget around that.

### 4. Get the school's real data
Pull from NCES, the state report card, and community demographics (poverty rate, median income). You need school-level AND community-level data. A reviewer lives in this community. Connect to their experience of it.

### 5. Identify the nearest facility (for Walmart Spark Good)
Look up the specific store address. Name it in the Local Connection section. The facility manager at THAT store reviews the application.

## Required Package Contents

Every application package must contain ALL of these:

### Welcome Section
- Personal greeting using the client's first name
- One sentence explaining what this grant funds (in plain English, tied to TDI services)
- Numbered list of what's inside the document
- "You do not need to write anything" statement
- Time estimate ("About 15 minutes")
- Help contact (Bella's email, offer to schedule a call)
- Note for administration: "If you need to share this with your principal for approval, everything they need is in this document"

### School Credentials Block
All credentials listed upfront so the client never has to look anything up:
- School name, district, NCES number, EIN
- Address, contact name, email, phone
- Nearest grant facility (Walmart store address, etc.)

### Step-by-Step Instructions
- Numbered steps with exact URLs
- Account setup instructions specific to the school type (public school via NCES, not 501c3)
- Guidance for if the school already has an existing account
- "If you hit any snag, email Bella. Do not spend time troubleshooting alone."
- What to do at each step if something unexpected happens

### Pre-Written Application Sections
Each section must have:
- The EXACT form field name as the header (not our label)
- Character limit shown
- Actual character count of our text shown (e.g., "497 of 500")
- "Paste into: [Field Name]" instruction
- Text verified to be within the limit

### Budget
- Every line item maps to a TDI contract service
- Verified pricing (matches the contract, not made up)
- Percentages shown
- "100% of funds" or clear breakdown

### Post-Award Section
- How the client selects which educators receive the service
- Activation timeline ("we activate within 48 hours")
- JP Morgan transfer setup instructions (if applicable)

### Post-Submission Section
- Expected review timeline
- What happens if approved (specific next steps)
- What happens if denied (we resubmit to different facility or pursue other grants)
- "Either way, we keep going" message
- Mention of other grants being pursued simultaneously

### Submission Checklist
- Printable checkbox format
- Every step from account setup through submission
- Last item: "Forward confirmation email to bella@teachersdeserveit.com"

## Writing Quality Standards

### Section 1 (Organization Overview): Tell a story, not facts
**Wrong:** "Allenwood Elementary is a pre-K through 5 school serving 400 students with 75 educators."
**Right:** "Allenwood Elementary sits in the heart of Temple Hills, where 400 children walk through our doors every morning counting on 75 educators to help them learn to read and do math."

### Section 2 (Community Need): Paint the community, not just the school
Include community demographics (poverty rate, median income). The reviewer LIVES here. Connect the school's data to what they see every day.
**Wrong:** "Only 27% of students read on grade level."
**Right:** "In Temple Hills, 12% of families live below the poverty line. At Allenwood, only 27% of students read on grade level."

### Section 3 (Use of Funds): Explain WHO gets the service and WHY them
Never just say "25 educators." Say which 25 and why: "Priority goes to K-2 literacy and math interventionists working directly with our lowest-performing students."

### Section 4 (Outcomes): Commit to specific student outcome targets
Never say "we will track proficiency." Say "We target moving reading proficiency from 27% to 35% and math from 5% to 12% by June 2027."

### Section 5 (Local Connection): Name the specific facility by address
"Allenwood is 2.5 miles from your Supercenter at 4317 St Barnabas Road." Not "the Temple Hills Walmart."

### Tone
Plain, conversational, confident. Short sentences. No jargon. No dashes. A store manager reads these, not an academic.

### Character limits
Write TO the limit. Every unused character is a missed opportunity. Show the count in the document.

## Grant-Specific Form Fields

### Walmart Spark Good (up to $5,000)
Five sections:
1. **Organization Overview** (500 chars)
2. **Community Need** (500 chars)
3. **Proposed Use of Funds** (500 chars)
4. **Expected Outcomes** (500 chars)
5. **Local Connection** (350 chars)

Evaluated by local facility manager on: Mission Aligned, Builds Trust, Effectiveness.
Schools verify via NCES number (no 501c3 needed). Verification through Deed platform (1-2 weeks).
Cannot reapply to same facility in same fiscal year.
If approved, JP Morgan transfer must be set up within 21 days.

### NEA Learning & Leadership (up to $5,000)
Written in FIRST PERSON from the NEA member teacher.
Sections: Lead Applicant, Activities, Need Assessment, Sustainability & Sharing, Budget (upload).
Project must NOT start before notification date (auto-reject).
Budget upload as Word, Excel, or PDF.

## Quality Gate: 20-Point Checklist

Before setting narrative_status to "review," verify ALL 20:

**Package completeness:**
- [ ] Welcome section with client's first name and what the grant funds
- [ ] "You do not need to write anything" statement
- [ ] Time estimate for submission
- [ ] Note for administration/principal sharing
- [ ] School credentials block (NCES, EIN, address, contact, facility)
- [ ] Step-by-step instructions with exact URLs
- [ ] Guidance for existing accounts
- [ ] Post-award logistics (how to select educators, activation timeline)
- [ ] Post-submission timeline (review period, approval steps, denial plan)
- [ ] Printable checklist with checkboxes

**Application quality:**
- [ ] Every section labeled with EXACT form field name
- [ ] Character counts verified and shown
- [ ] Budget funds TDI contract services only (not unrelated items)
- [ ] Grant amount is the maximum allowed
- [ ] Section 1 has emotional hook (story, not factsheet)
- [ ] Section 2 includes community demographics (poverty, income) from census data
- [ ] Section 3 explains which educators and why them
- [ ] Section 4 commits to specific student outcome targets with numbers
- [ ] Section 5 names the specific facility by street address
- [ ] No dashes, no jargon, no passive voice

**Verification (do these BEFORE marking as review):**
- [ ] Every statistic verified against its source (NCES, state report card, census). If a number cannot be verified, flag it for Bella to confirm with the school.
- [ ] Budget price per unit matches a documented TDI rate (contract price, bulk rate, or standard rate) with justification for which rate is used
- [ ] Document is accessible: open the link in an incognito browser. If it asks for access, fix sharing before sending.
- [ ] Read the complete package as if you are the reviewer. Does it make you want to fund this school immediately? If not, rewrite until it does.
- [ ] Read the complete package as if you are the client (Teri, Paula, Gary). Is every step obvious? Would you know exactly what to do at every point? If any step requires thinking, clarify it.
