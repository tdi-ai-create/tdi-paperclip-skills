# TDI Platform Feature Audit
**Last updated:** July 20, 2026
**Purpose:** Complete inventory of all user-facing features across the Learning Hub and Leadership Dashboard. Use this as the canonical reference when creating content, writing copy, answering questions about what TDI offers, or planning marketing materials.

---

## PART 1: LEARNING HUB FEATURES (32 features)

### Wellbeing and Self-Care (3)

1. **"I Need a Moment" Mode**
   - Solves: When you're overwhelmed mid-day and need a reset without leaving the building
   - What it is: Guided breathing, journaling, affirmations accessible from any page via the heart icon
   - Access: All tiers

2. **Capacity Feedback Prompts**
   - Solves: Platform checks in on YOUR bandwidth before suggesting more work
   - What it is: Contextual prompts that ask "How's your capacity?" and adapt recommendations
   - Access: All tiers

3. **Wellbeing Tracking**
   - Solves: Having a hard day? The hub notices and responds gently
   - What it is: Aggregated wellbeing data that triggers supportive responses, not more tasks
   - Access: All tiers

### Learning and Growth (9)

4. **Course Library**
   - Solves: PD that fits YOUR schedule, not a mandatory after-school session
   - What it is: On-demand courses with video, transcripts, progress tracking, and PD hour credits
   - Categories: Stress & Wellness, Classroom Management, Leadership, Communication, Games
   - Access: Tiered (some free, full library at All-Access)

5. **Quick Wins Library**
   - Solves: Need something you can use tomorrow morning? Here it is.
   - What it is: Downloadable PDF tools sorted by topic, time, and effort level
   - Categories: Instructional Strategies, Lesson Planning, Assessment, Classroom Setup, Management, Communication, Time Savers, Leadership, Self-Care, Stress Relief, Games, Vocational
   - Access: Rotating free weekly, full library at Professional+

6. **Practice Games (11 types)**
   - Solves: Practice real classroom skills without the pressure of a real classroom
   - Games: Question Knockout, Tell or Ask?, Feedback Level Up, Feedback Madlibs, Feedback Makeover, What's Your Move?, Classroom Scenario Shuffle, Prioritize This, Energy Budget, This or That, Sort It Out
   - Access: Professional+

7. **Personalized Recommendations**
   - Solves: Stop scrolling. The hub suggests what fits your goals.
   - What it is: Algorithm-based content suggestions adapted to your role, goals, and progress
   - Access: All tiers

8. **Goal-Based Learning**
   - Solves: You pick what matters. Everything else adapts.
   - What it is: Select goals (reduce stress, save time, classroom management, find joy, team growth) and content aligns
   - Access: All tiers

9. **Lesson Q&A and Conversations**
   - Solves: Questions mid-lesson? Ask and get answers without leaving.
   - What it is: In-lesson question threads and discussion conversations
   - Access: All tiers with course access

10. **Practice Notes**
    - Solves: Tried something in your classroom? Save what worked.
    - What it is: Personal notes created after games and lessons, shareable with community
    - Access: All tiers

11. **Continue Where You Left Off**
    - Solves: Picked up your phone between classes? Jump right back in.
    - What it is: Dashboard shows your last course, last lesson position, and next steps
    - Access: All tiers

12. **Content Filtering and Search**
    - Solves: Find exactly what you need without browsing everything
    - What it is: Filter by category, saved items, access tier; global search across all content types
    - Access: All tiers

### Recognition and Proof (4)

13. **PD Certificates**
    - Solves: Need proof for your admin? Here's a verifiable certificate with real PD hours.
    - What it is: Downloadable, shareable certificates with unique verification codes
    - Access: All-Access tier

14. **Recognition Celebrations (Field Notes)**
    - Solves: Your effort gets celebrated, not just tracked
    - What it is: Milestone-based celebrations. Examples: Hub Pioneer (first login), First Step (first tool downloaded), Toolkit Builder (5 tools explored), Self-Care Champion (5 uses of Moment Mode), Rising Voice (first community share)
    - Access: All tiers

15. **Growth Tracker**
    - Solves: See your own transformation over time
    - What it is: Visual dashboard tracking stress trends, completions, goals progress, and milestones
    - Access: Eligible educators (conditional)

16. **Game Badges and Stats**
    - Solves: Proof you're building skills, not just watching videos
    - What it is: Earned badges and performance stats from practice games with celebration animations
    - Access: Professional+

### Community and Connection (3)

17. **Community Space**
    - Solves: See how other educators applied the same tool in THEIR classroom
    - What it is: Shared space for classroom experiences, likes, bookmarks, comments, and search
    - Access: Essentials+

18. **Request Center**
    - Solves: Ask for what you actually need. Vote on what others requested.
    - What it is: Submit and track requests for new courses, quick wins, workshops, and resources
    - Access: All tiers

19. **Universal Sharing**
    - Solves: Show the world what you're learning
    - What it is: Share certificates, quiz results, and profile cards to Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook, email, or clipboard
    - Access: All tiers

### Personalization (5)

20. **Educator Type Quiz**
    - Solves: Find out what kind of educator you are
    - What it is: 5-question quiz resulting in one of 5 types: Architect, Connector, Innovator, Anchor, or Spark
    - Access: All tiers

21. **Onboarding Flow**
    - Solves: The hub learns YOU first before suggesting anything
    - What it is: Multi-step setup: select role (Teacher, Para, Coach, Leader, District, Other), select goals, take quiz, choose avatar
    - Access: All tiers (first login)

22. **Avatar Selection**
    - Solves: Make it feel like yours
    - What it is: Choose from illustrated avatars (owl, coffee mug, plant, etc.) or upload your own
    - Access: All tiers

23. **Multi-Language Support**
    - Solves: Use the platform in your language
    - What it is: Full EN/ES toggle across the entire interface (lesson body content English-only for now)
    - Access: All tiers

24. **Notification Preferences**
    - Solves: Control how and when the hub reaches out
    - What it is: Set email frequency, preferred learning day/time, quiet hours, popup toggles
    - Access: All tiers

### School Leader Tools in Hub (3)

25. **Team Engagement View**
    - Solves: See who's using PD without micromanaging
    - What it is: Enrollment status by educator (no private progress data exposed)
    - Access: School admin/champion role

26. **Org-Level Analytics**
    - Solves: PD hours and completions across your building
    - What it is: Team statistics: total members, enrolled, not enrolled, completed courses
    - Access: School admin/champion role

27. **Course Recommendations for Team**
    - Solves: Push relevant PD to your whole team in one click
    - What it is: Add/remove recommended courses visible to all team members
    - Access: School admin/champion role

### Access and Convenience (5)

28. **Tiered Membership**
    - Solves: Start free, upgrade when you're ready
    - Tiers: Free (rotating weekly content), Essentials ($5/mo: expanded Quick Wins, PDFs, community), Professional ($10/mo: full library, games, monthly drops), All-Access ($25/mo: full courses, certificates, workshops, coaching)
    - Access: All

29. **Add to Home Screen (PWA)**
    - Solves: Access from your phone like an app
    - What it is: Progressive web app prompt for mobile home screen installation
    - Access: All tiers

30. **Global Search**
    - Solves: Find anything across the entire platform
    - What it is: Search across courses, quick wins, and community content with type filtering
    - Access: All tiers

31. **Shareable Profile Cards**
    - Solves: Show off your growth
    - What it is: Downloadable cards with quiz results and achievements for social sharing
    - Access: All tiers

32. **Certificate Verification**
    - Solves: Your admin can verify your PD is legit
    - What it is: Public verification page using unique certificate codes
    - Access: Public (anyone with the code)

    - Solves: Track adoption over time without chasing people down
    - What it is: Line chart of Hub login activity across the team
    - Audience: Partner school leaders

35. **Course Completion Funnel**
    - Solves: See where educators drop off so you can support, not mandate
    - What it is: Visual funnel from enrollment to completion
    - Audience: Partner school leaders

36. **Course Recommendations**
    - Solves: Push relevant PD to your whole team in one click
    - What it is: Recommend courses visible to all team members
    - Audience: Partner school leaders

37. **Enrollment Status View**
    - Solves: Know who's in and who's not without invading anyone's privacy
    - What it is: Team view showing enrollment only, not individual progress or scores
    - Audience: Partner school leaders

38. **Partnership Timeline**
    - Solves: See every observation, meeting, and milestone in one place
    - What it is: Chronological timeline of all partnership events
    - Audience: Partner school leaders

39. **Momentum Bar**
    - Solves: Visual progress indicator, no spreadsheet needed
    - What it is: Single progress bar showing overall partnership health
    - Audience: Partner school leaders

40. **Leading Indicators**
    - Solves: Track what actually predicts teacher retention
    - What it is: Metrics: teacher stress levels, strategy implementation, retention intent
    - Audience: Partner school leaders

41. **Investment / ROI Metrics**
    - Solves: Show your board the return on your PD investment
    - What it is: Financial and impact data formatted for stakeholder reporting
    - Audience: Partner school leaders

42. **Love Notes Callout**
    - Solves: Hear what's actually landing with your team
    - What it is: Aggregated educator feedback highlights
    - Audience: Partner school leaders

43. **Section Highlights**
    - Solves: Focus on what matters most to your school right now
    - What it is: Customizable dashboard sections controlled by TDI
    - Audience: Partner school leaders

### TDI Internal / Partnership Management (14)

44. **Partnership Pipeline**
    - Solves: Track every school from first contact through onboarding to active
    - What it is: Multi-status pipeline with filtering by type, status, and phase

45. **Onboarding Checklist**
    - Solves: No school launches unprepared
    - What it is: Activation readiness gate with required checklist items

46. **Activation Readiness Score**
    - Solves: One number that tells you if a school is ready to go live
    - What it is: Calculated readiness score based on setup completion

47. **KPI Management**
    - Solves: Set targets, track benchmarks, measure what matters per school
    - What it is: Curated KPI menu with customizable targets per partnership

48. **Cross-Partnership Action Items**
    - Solves: Never lose a follow-up across dozens of schools
    - What it is: Centralized task tracking with priority, status, and due dates

49. **Observation Impact Scorecard**
    - Solves: Prove coaching sessions work with before/after data
    - What it is: Before and after metrics from classroom observations

50. **Internal Notes (Visibility Controls)**
    - Solves: Keep internal context separate from what the school sees
    - What it is: Notes with a visible-to-partner flag for controlled sharing

51. **Meeting Log**
    - Solves: Track every conversation, attendee, and action item
    - What it is: Structured meeting records linked to partnerships

52. **AI Document Extraction**
    - Solves: Upload a contract or grant doc and pull structured data automatically
    - What it is: AI-powered parsing of uploaded documents into structured fields

53. **Service Deliverables Tracking**
    - Solves: Know what's been delivered and what's pending
    - What it is: Contract line items with delivery status, direct pay vs grant funded

54. **Staff Photo Management**
    - Solves: Keep school rosters looking professional
    - What it is: Individual and bulk photo upload for team members

55. **Portal Invite System**
    - Solves: Get school leaders set up in one click
    - What it is: Automated invite emails with portal access and login tracking

56. **School Reports**
    - Solves: Live engagement data without manual reporting
    - What it is: Hub engagement metrics aggregated by building

57. **Billing and Contract Tracking**
    - Solves: Session usage, renewals, and allocations in one view
    - What it is: Contract period tracking with service usage and renewal alerts
